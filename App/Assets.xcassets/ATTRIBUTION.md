App icon (`AppIcon.appiconset/icon-1024.png`) and connect mark
(`AppLogo.imageset/AppLogo.png`) replace the Herdrup sheep artwork from

`com.elysium.herdrcompanion` client. They are not operational screenshots.

Bundled Geist and IBM Plex Mono fonts are unchanged from that Herdrup
baseline; see the SIL Open Font License files in `App/Fonts/`.
